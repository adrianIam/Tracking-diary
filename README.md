# Tracking Diary

A simple headache and migraine diary for recording pain and noticing patterns over time.

[Open Tracking Diary](https://adrianiam.github.io/Tracking-diary/dist/)

## Record pain

1. Press and hold the record button for each burst of pain.
2. Release when the burst ends. Repeat as needed within the same session.
3. Select **Add details & save**, choose headache or migraine and the intensity, then save.

Each session keeps the individual burst times and total pain duration, excluding the gaps between bursts. You can optionally add symptoms, possible triggers, relief measures, medication, and notes.

You can also log an episode manually, enter its timing, or leave it ongoing and end it later.

## Review your diary

- **My diary:** search, filter, edit, or delete saved entries.
- **Insights:** view summaries and intensity-coloured charts for daily pain duration and frequency throughout the day. The duration chart follows the summary period; the frequency chart has its own time range and uses 20-minute intervals.
- **Data & settings:** download or import a backup, export a CSV, and find instructions for adding a diary icon to your phone’s home screen.

## Keep your records safe

Your entries stay in the browser on your device and do not automatically sync elsewhere. Download backups regularly, especially before changing devices or clearing browser data. Unsaved recording sessions are lost if you reload or close the page.
